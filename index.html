About the Advocate

Aarav Mehta is an advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi in 2012. He practises in civil, commercial and criminal law, appearing before the High Court of Delhi, district courts and tribunals in Delhi.

He is a member of the Delhi High Court Bar Association and holds a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) from a National Law University and an LL.M. in Commercial Law.
