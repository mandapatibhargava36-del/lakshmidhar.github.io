About the Advocate

Kotha Lakshmidhar is an advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Andhra Pradesh in 2012. He practises in civil, commercial and criminal law, appearing before the High Court of Andhra Pradesh, district courts and tribunals in Andhra Pradesh.

He is a member of the High Court Advocates Association, Andhra Pradesh and holds a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) from a National Law University and an LL.M. in Commercial Law.

Practice

Areas of Practice

Fields of law in which the advocate practises. This is a factual listing, not a claim of specialisation or expertise.

Civil Law

Civil suits, property and tenancy matters, injunctions, and execution proceedings under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.

Criminal Law

Matters under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

Commercial Law

Commercial disputes, contracts and arbitration under the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 and the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Family Law

Matrimonial, maintenance, guardianship and succession matters under applicable personal and secular laws.

Writ Jurisdiction

Petitions under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India before the High Court.

Consumer & Cheque Matters

Proceedings under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.
